\begin{abstract}
A language model that learns tasks one after another through adapters forgets, because every new
adapter changes what the model computes on old inputs. FK-LoRA gives every task one frozen set of
adapters and sends each input to one of them; an answer can then change only if its route changes,
so retention is a property of the rule that sets the route, and can be proved or measured. Our
main rule reads no instruction. It is a recogniser on frozen states of the model; it blocks no
task, and it comes with an audit, which bounds the share of changed answers from held-out inputs,
and a handicap, which limits in advance what a later task can take. A simple bound shows that,
without instructions, any method loses on two tasks together at least the share
of inputs that they have in common with different right answers. Benchmarks hide this, because
their inputs begin with the instruction of the task: without it the two published methods that we
ran change \AudSeedsGainloraChanged{} and \AudSeedsInfloraChanged{} of \AudSeedsCompared{} answers
(\FiveGainChanged{} and \FiveInfChanged{} with it). This loss was not forced: no benchmark input
occurs in two tasks, and the recogniser gives \AudDevLLamOneEnd{}\% of the test inputs of 15
benchmark tasks to their task (development data), \AudScaleLongThreeEnd{}\% on a second benchmark
and \AudFreshFourthLEnd{}\% on 30 unseen tasks. Trained without instructions on one sequence of
five of these tasks (three seeds), it changes \AudSeedsRidgeChanged{} of \AudSeedsCompared{}
answers and ends at \AudSeedsRidgeAPOne{} Rouge-L against \AudSeedsGainloraAPOne{} and
\AudSeedsInfloraAPOne{} for the published methods, which ran with a tenth of their epochs; most of
that gap is learning. With their full epochs (one seed) they learn the tasks and then forget
\AudFullGainloraFTInt{} and \AudFullInfloraFTInt{} points, ending at \AudFullGainloraAPOne{} and
\AudFullInfloraAPOne{}, where ours ends at \AudFullOursRidgeAPOne{} with the same epochs; with instructions they forget \AudFullDefOriginalGainloraFTOne{} and \AudFullDefOriginalInfloraFTOne{}. When inputs carry an instruction, a second rule is exact: each task
claims a zone around the frozen word-vectors of its instruction, and we prove that the answer to an
input inside a zone never changes (\FiveCertChanged{} of \FiveCertCompared{} and
\FifteenCertChanged{} of \FifteenCertCompared{} answers changed on SuperNI with T5-base); reworded
instructions leave the zones (\WidenRefTfivePLow{}\% to \WidenRefTfivePHigh{}\% certified for the
right task on machine paraphrases of \WidenTasksEval{} tasks). Nothing here is in the published
setting.
\end{abstract}
